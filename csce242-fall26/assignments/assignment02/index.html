A Profesional and Social Sorority for Women in STEM!

In our Sorority we focus on building leadership, friendship, and professional development. We are open to women in STEM majors, though any major is welcome! The UofSC Alpha Omega Epsilon chapter was founded in April of 2019. AOE is continuing to expand and grow both in and out of school! Alpha Omega Epsilon prides itself in the commmunity service, networking, and sistserhood we have. Every year we work with organizations at UofSc, around Columbia, and nationally to help those in need. AOE is also a great oppertunity for networking. You need connection in the industry and we are here to help you get them! Most importantly is sisterhood. Our sisters are uplifting, encouraging, and always there for one another. Come join AOE and meet your best friends today!

Recruitment Events

this semester's recruitment theme is... UNDER THE SEA WITH AOE!

The recruitment process this year will work a little differently than in the past. This year we will have 3 weeks of recruitment. Week one will host info nights so that you can learn more about our sorority. Week two we will have 1 social event, 1 philanthropy, and one PD. Here you will get a chnace to meet and connect with current sisters of AOE. The final week we will hold interviews and an invite only event so that we can decide who are the best fits for our sorority! We hope to see you there!!

GIGA COASTERS IN AMERICA

    Current Running Giga Coasters in America:

  • Fury 325 - Carowinds NC
  • Millennium Force - Cedar Point OH
  • Pantherian(Intimidator 305) - Kings Dominion VA
  • Orion - Kings Island OH
  • Tormenta Rampaging Run - Six Flags Over Texas TX

Fury 325

Fury 325 debuted at Carowinds as the tallest giga coaster in 2015. The Fury resides on the North Carolina side of Carowinds in the "Thrill Zone". It is a steel coaster and features 6602 feet of track. Fury 325 has been awarded a Golden Ticket Award for the past 5 years (2021-2025). The ride is largely respected as one of the world's best roller coasters today!

Millennium Force

Millenium Force opened at Cedar point in 2000 as the world's very first giga coaster. It broke 5 records upon opening. It has won 10 Golden Ticket Awards for best steel coaster. The coaster has drops, tunnels and inversions. While it is not the tallest or fastest coaster anymore, it carved the way for other giga coasters.

Welcome to ARTSCOVERY!!!

This is a place to learn, create, and connect through art! Whether it's your first time picking up a pencil or you've been creating for years here you can find tutorials, resources, contest, and a community full of artists like you!

Learn new techniques, discover inspiration, and begin to love creating. There is no "right" way to start, so let's start TOGETHER!

Our Mission:

Art is for EVERYONE. While artistic ability is often viewed as an innate talent, I believe it is a skill that can be learned and developed over time. Regardless of age, vailable materials, or personal setbacks, creating art is always within reach. My goal is to help people engage with art at every stage of their learning journey. Whether they pursue it as a hobby or a career. Starting somewhere is always more valuable than not starting at all. I want to help others not only learn to create, but also learn to love what they create.
